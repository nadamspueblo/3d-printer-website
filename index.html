How it works / 03

Built through trial and error.

01

Design

We sketch the first idea, then turn it into a 3D model and hope it doesn’t explode in the printer.

02

Print

We prep the file, hit print, and see if the layers come out clean or if the whole thing starts wobbling.

03

Improve

Then we fix the weak spots, tweak the details, and make the next version way better than the first one.

About the project / 04

Student-built.
Made one layer at a time.

Layer & Form is a 3D-printing project made by Pueblo High School students. We’re learning how ideas become real objects, testing what works, and making cool stuff together as the project grows.

What we’re learning

  • 3D design basics
